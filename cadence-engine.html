Kings Research cadence engine, v8.39

From a prospect list to four emails that sell the research itself

Every row in the sheet passes through ten steps. People who are not a fit, and companies that are not who the sheet says they are, are stopped early and for free. The ones who reach writing get emails built from a real Kings Research report page or offering, every sentence is checked before the row is marked Ready, and every email closes on a question built from the research that was matched, not on a request for a call.

      Value-led closing lines

      Every email ends on a question built from four facts the engine already holds for the prospect: the company signal, the matched report or offering, the finding E1 uses, and the person's role. The line is assembled by code, not written by a model, so it cannot promise a capability the match does not have, and nothing is ever offered for free. The connect link in E4 stays.

      What the line may say

      Value only from the match
      Report rows: the page's own coverage and the finding card E1 uses. Offering rows: the family table and the study actually matched. No capability the match does not have.
      No free deliverables
      Every "one page", "summary" and "single page" line is gone; in-body page promises become "The full analysis also covers …".
      Report versus custom research
      "the full [Market] market analysis" for report rows; "a [Study] on the …" for offering rows.
      A real market name, or the company
      Starts with "the", longer than four words, or contains the company name: not a market. The lines are built around the company instead.

      When the line is not applied

      Clean text or nothing
      A line with stray brackets, odd characters or a broken list is never applied.
      Referral cadences
      Rows that ask for the right person keep their own closing.
      Unknown shapes
      A closing line the writer produced in a different shape is left as written, not guessed at.
      Everywhere Ready is written
      Runner, promotions, the Ready guard and the ten-minute sweep run the same code. A rule change re-checks every Ready row automatically; the final reader still reads the finished emails before Ready.

      The guard rails

      These hold every time. A row that breaks one is held with the reason, the evidence and the exact text, so it can be fixed or the rule corrected.

      Never in an email

      Anything offered for free
      No one-pager, sample, report, deck or download. Every email ends on a value-led question built from the matched report or offering (see Closing lines); E4 adds the link to our team.
      Numbers and links before E4
      No figures, percentages, amounts or years. A news opener that only works with its number is dropped. Share-price and stock headlines are never an opener. The link to our team appears once, at the end of E4.
      Words the page does not back
      A finding keeps only what the report page contains: a tail clause the page does not have ("which may impact service delivery") is cut. "Our latest analysis" only when the page's base year is last year or newer.
      Claims and advice about the reader
      Never says they have a problem or are behind. What a finding means is always a question it raises. "Should", "must" and "needs to" in the opening question become "could". No product the company does not describe.
      Sentences that point at nothing
      "The segment", "this rapid growth", "these standards", "the region", "the high share" unnamed: replaced with the page's own sentence, or the region is named from the card; a dangling finding holds the row.
      Other company names and jargon
      Competitors named on the page are blocked; places such as "North America" are not mistaken for them. "Leveraging" becomes "using"; "groundbreaking" and "lucrative" are dropped; "robust" becomes "strong".

      Shape of every cadence

      E1
      Greeting, opening question, what the report covers (at most two breakdowns, two examples each, only ones the page lists), the first finding, what it could show this person, and a closing question built from the E1 finding and the role.
      E2
      A finding of its own, a question that shares a word with it and is built from its subject, and a closing question on the report's coverage.
      E3
      A finding of its own, from a card E1 and E2 did not use. A coverage-only line is never sent; with nothing left on the page the row is held. Closes on the full market analysis (or the matched study).
      E4
      A commercial close ("Would it make sense to reconnect about the … analysis when this becomes a priority?"), then the link to our team.
      Three findings or hold
      A report page needs three findings that fit the company (MIN_FINDINGS=3), one for each of E1, E2 and E3, or the row is held before writing.
      Colleagues
      Two contacts at one account never share a news opener, the E1 coverage line, the E1 finding or the E2 finding; the same opening line to two contacts holds the row.

      Who gets a cadence

      Buyers, not browsers
      Influencers below VP, client-facing sales roles, engineers and scientists without a leadership title, field sales VPs and communications-only roles are held.
      Technical VPs
      Get a referral cadence: the same insight, and an ask for a call or the right person.
      Two per account
      At most two Ready contacts per account across every batch; a parent and its companies count as one (config/parents.csv).
      Real companies, real people
      Spam websites, websites that name another company, non-profits, acquired companies and competitors are stopped before any spend. A first name that does not match the email address is held before matching.

      Truth and spend

      Traced to a source
      Report sentences must share their words with the page and agree with it on not, no and less. Findings in config/bad-findings.txt are never used.
      Fact guard, then an audit of the guard
      Code checks every flag before a row is held. A flag on the E1 finding is repaired with the page's own sentence and checked again. A flag dismissed as "an open question" is kept when it sits in a finding sentence. Guard edits never touch our own template or question lines.
      Quality review on quoted issues
      Every quote is judged on its whole sentence. An "irrelevant" issue on a page finding that Jev scored as relevant to the company is overruled. If the review cannot run, the row is held.
      Ready only with all four emails
      Nothing reaches column N as Ready without a subject and four emails already in place and read back (v8.34, v8.35). The self-audit re-reads every Ready row whenever a rule changes, and a final reader checks for wrong facts, wrong company or broken sentences before Ready (v8.37, v8.38).
      Spend limits
      COST_CAP_PER_1K=0.80 is enforced: over it, the rest of the run is held before any paid step.

      The cases we handled to make it robust

      Every case started as a real row that went wrong in a run. Each is now handled by code and has a regression test built from that row: the installer runs every test and puts every file back if one fails.

      What each status means

      Ready
      Passed every content check: the rule checks, the fact guard, the quality review, the self-audit and the final reader. It does not mean sent.
      Hold: reason
      Held, with the reason. A written draft is kept in the Dossier, not in the email columns; the Dossier starts with a hold audit: what failed, the evidence checked and why. Holds made before writing spent nothing more on the row.
      Retry: reason
      Sent back by the self-audit for a fresh run through every check, at most twice. Picked up automatically on the next run.
      Rejected
      Failed a free check, such as a denied title or a disqualified account. Nothing was spent.
      Not a fit
      The organisation is not a buyer of commissioned research, such as a non-profit or public body.
      Sent
      Never set by the engine. Only your sending tool sends.

      What it costs

      The target is $0.80 per 1,000 prospects, counting everyone in the run. Rows stopped before writing cost close to nothing; a written and checked cadence costs about a sixth of a cent. Move the sliders to size a run.
